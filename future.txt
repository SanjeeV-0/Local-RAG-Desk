3. Should this be an LLM call at all?
Your existing query_router.py deliberately does not use an LLM for its decompose/don't-decompose decision — it's a fast, deterministic, rule-based classifier, precisely to avoid adding latency and the flaky-model risk to every single request. The bigger issue: none of these three retrieval strategies exist yet

This is the part that matters most. The prompt assumes three backend retrieval modes are available to dispatch to — none of them are implemented in this codebase today:
